DIAGNOSIS (the informative part): the surviving completions are CONES -- an

Blocking every cone requires forcing unions OUT of P, and the patterns pin

STRUCTURAL CONCLUSION: every constraint in the toolkit -- Oliver chi
conditions, Smith acyclicity, monotone propagation -- pushes graphs INTO P;
the only OUT-generator is nontriviality (K_10 not in P).  The topological
method is one-sided.  KSS wins at prime powers because a transitive group's
lattice is coarse enough that IN-forcing + the single top OUT are jointly

propagate down to the IN-forced region, and all 18 patterns live in the

a derivation, from non-evasiveness, that specific dense-but-incomplete graphs
